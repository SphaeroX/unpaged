PDFs are made for paper. Unpaged makes them readable on your phone.

Open any PDF in its original layout, or switch to the eBook mode: papers, textbooks and technical documents are reflowed in your font, your size and your theme. Tables, figures and formulas stay sharp. Everything happens offline on your phone.

See it in action

One tap from page to eBook

Open a two-column paper, tap the eBook button, and read. Figures stay exactly as they were in the original.

Features

Made for papers, textbooks and technical documents

Unpaged understands the layout of a page: columns, headings, captions and the parts that are not text.

eBook mode

PDFs with a text layer are reflowed like an eBook, completely offline, including two-column layouts.

Clean reading flow

Reading order across columns, running headers and page numbers removed, hyphenated words joined again.

Figures stay sharp

Tables, figures and formulas are shown as crops of the original page. Tap to zoom.

Original view

The PDF exactly as published, with smooth zooming. Switch views with one tap.

Your reading settings

Literata, Atkinson Hyperlegible or the system font, text size, line spacing, light, dark and sepia.

Highlights and notes

Mark text and add notes in both views. They stay in the app; your PDF is never changed.

Library with progress

Continue where you stopped. Import with the file picker, “Open with” or “Share” from any app.

Share and print

Hand the original PDF to any app or printer, only when you choose to.

Private by design

Your documents never leave your phone

Unpaged does not even have permission to access the internet. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing that phones home.

Read the privacy policy

  • No network access
  • No account, no ads
  • No analytics, no crash reporter
  • Open source: check it yourself

Good to know: scanned PDFs without a text layer open in the original view, but cannot be reflowed, because Unpaged does no text recognition (OCR).
